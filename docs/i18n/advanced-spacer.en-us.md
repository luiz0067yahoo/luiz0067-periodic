# Periodic Advanced Spacer

> Responsive vertical spacer with millimeter height control per device (desktop, tablet and mobile), native Bootstrap 5 visibility rules and elegant decorative dividers with central icons.

---

## 📋 Overview

The default WordPress spacer block is a fixed height, which often causes excessive or broken spacing on smartphones and tablets. **Periodic Advanced Spacer** solves this structural limitation by allowing you to define custom heights for each breakpoint (*breakpoint*), strategically hide the block on specific devices via Bootstrap 5 classes, and optionally transform it into a sophisticated decorative dividing line.

---

## ✨ Main Features

- **Triple Responsive Height Control**: Set different heights for Desktop (≥ 992px), Tablet (768px to 991px) and Mobile (< 768px).
- **Visual Simulator in Editor**: Switch between Desktop, Tablet and Mobile with one click in the panel to visually inspect the result without resizing the browser window.
- **Bootstrap 5 Visibility Rules**: Hide the spacer on large screens (`d-lg-none`), on tablets (`d-md-none`) or on cell phones (`d-none d-sm-block`).
- **Optional Dividing Lines**: Convert the spacing to a solid, dashed, dotted, or luxury gradient divider.
- **Center Decorative Icons**: Add decorative symbols in the center of the dividing line (star ★, diamond ◆, or circle ●).

---

## 🎛️ Control Panel Tabs (InspectorControls)

### 1. Heights Tab
- **Visual Simulation**: Buttons to toggle instant preview in the editor.
- **Desktop Height**: Slider adjustment from 0px to 300px for computers and wide monitors.
- **Tablet Height**: Slider adjustment from 0px to 200px for iPads and tablets.
- **Mobile Height**: Slider adjustment from 0px to 150px for smartphone screens.

### 2. Visibility Tab
- **Hide on Desktop**: Applies the Bootstrap 5 `d-lg-none` class.
- **Hide on Tablet**: Hides in the medium responsive range.
- **Hide on Mobile**: Applies mobile conditional display classes.

### 3. Dividing Line Tab
- **Line Style**: None (invisible space), Solid, Dashed, Dotted, or Smooth Gradient.
- **Line Color**: Integrated palette to harmonize with the visual identity of the theme.
- **Thickness & Width**: Control from 1px to 10px in height and from 10% to 100% in horizontal width.
- **Alignment**: Align the divider to the left, center or right.
- **Decorative Icon**: Choose between star, diamond or central circle.
